setup. They are **not a redistribution license**. Public availability and a

[NVIDIA terms](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/about-nvidia/terms-of-service/)
section 3.1 describes downloading one copy on a single computer for personal,
noncommercial internal use. Its restrictions remain applicable; section 17
separately reserves trademark permissions. The current
[logo and brand guidance](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/about-nvidia/legal-info/logo-brand-usage/)
requires written authorization for branded elements. These pages do not grant
a general right to redistribute NVIDIA artwork or logos with Silicon Quest.

[Coursera's January 2026 terms](https://www.coursera.org/about/terms)
grant a personal, noncommercial right to use its services, while its Acceptable
Use Policy restricts reproduction without authorization. A distinct standalone
artwork or redistribution license for the FPGA thumbnail was not found. The
[CU Boulder trademark policy](https://www.colorado.edu/compliance/policies/student-guide-using-trademarks)
also requires prior permission for marks. No CU mark is needed for the chip
illustration candidate.

Keep any locally installed copies, screenshots containing them, and backups
containing them private. Do not copy them into `public/`, `src/`, release
resources, documentation images, fixtures, or the Git repository. If distributing
the app, retain the neutral fallback and let each user import artwork they are
entitled to use. A generic learning-path image, provider visual, or course
thumbnail does not verify course availability, completion, or an earned badge.
